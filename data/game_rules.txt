[font_size=20][b]The goal[/b][/font_size]
Two islands face each other across the sea. Each side has {bases} bases. Destroy all of the enemy's bases before they destroy yours. If both sides lose their last base in the same round, the side with more money wins; with equal money, a coin decides.

[font_size=20][b]Setup[/b][/font_size]
Place your {bases} bases on your island. Bases and decoys may not touch each other. You may also buy decoys for {decoy_cost} money each: to the enemy a decoy looks exactly like a base. You start with {start_money} money and {start_fuel} fuel.

[font_size=20][b]Each round[/b][/font_size]
[b]1. Build and repair[/b] ({build_clock}). From round {income_from} on, income arrives first: {baseline_money} money and {baseline_fuel} fuel, plus what your buildings make. Build, repair, and choose research.
[b]2. Targeting[/b] ({targeting_clock}). Pick a launcher, then click a hex on the enemy island to aim it. Each launcher fires one missile a round, if you have the fuel.
[b]3. Playout.[/b] Both sides' missiles fly at once and anti-air shoots some of them down. All damage is applied at the end of the round, so a building destroyed this round still fires this round.

[font_size=20][b]Missiles[/b][/font_size]
{missile_list}
A missile reveals the hexes around where it lands, out to its view radius. Impacts don't reveal higher ground, and a blast doesn't cross a step in height.

[font_size=20][b]Bases and decoys[/b][/font_size]
A base falls at {base_hits} hits and a decoy at {decoy_hits}. A basic missile counts as one hit and a ballistic missile as half a hit. On the enemy island, potential bases show the hits they have taken; when a decoy falls, you learn it was a decoy.

[font_size=20][b]Anti-air and radar[/b][/font_size]
Anti-air shoots at missiles aimed within {aa_radius} hexes of it, up to {aa_engagements} a round ({aa_engagements_up} with research). It hits {acc}% of the time, or {acc_radar}% inside a radar's range. Radar reaches {radar_ranges} hexes from the base, middle and top level.

[font_size=20][b]Troops[/b][/font_size]
An airport (research it first) sends one air transport a round, for {transport_fuel} fuel and {transport_money} money. Aim it with two clicks: the landing hex, then a hex next to it for the direction of the march. Anti-air can shoot the transport down like a missile; a lost transport costs {transport_replace} money to replace, in the build and repair phase, before that airport can launch again.
The platoon lands and marches {march} more hexes in a straight line, {march_total} in all, destroying whatever stands on each, a base included. Platoons can jump down a height step but can't climb one; the sea or the edge of the island ends the march, and a platoon that lands in the sea is lost. A bunker wipes out a platoon that lands or marches within {bunker_range} hexes of it. Every hex a platoon reaches is revealed.

[font_size=20][b]Damage and repair[/b][/font_size]
Buildings have hit points, and missile damage is rolled. A damaged weapon is out of action until it is repaired; other buildings work at half strength while damaged. A repair costs {repair_pct}% of the building's price and takes the building out of action for that round; it is back to full the round after. Bases and decoys can't be repaired.

[font_size=20][b]Research[/b][/font_size]
Each lab makes {lab_points} research points a round toward its own project. Starting a project costs money; a lab without a project helps the oldest one. A project whose lab is destroyed keeps its progress.
{research_list}

[font_size=20][b]Fog of war[/b][/font_size]
The enemy island is hidden until your missiles reveal it, and what you saw may have changed since. The enemy sees your island the same way; the hexes it has seen are drawn lighter.
